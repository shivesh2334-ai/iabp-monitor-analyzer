Document-guided assessment

Adapted from the article’s clinical topics and timing figures (not a validated scoring tool). All inputs are optional. Enter what is known in Parameters, or upload an image in Image analysis and extract values for verification.

  1. Understand physiology and treatment goal.
  2. Review indication, contraindications and updated evidence.
  3. Verify position, balloon size, signal and trigger.
  4. Review inflation at the dicrotic notch.
  5. Review deflation before systolic ejection.
  6. Compare pressures and organ-perfusion response.
  7. Check complications and escalation needs.
  8. Review recovery, weaning and documentation.

Blank values remain unknown. Extracted values require review; image interpretation cannot confirm timing when landmarks are unclear.
